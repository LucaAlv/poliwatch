# The Präsident, Vizepräsidenten and the Alterspräsident have no Sprechrolle of their own

A member of the Bundestag who holds the office of Präsident, Vizepräsident(in) or Alterspräsident is not given an additional Sprechrolle because of that office. When they take part in a debate as an Abgeordneter, their Rede counts for their Zusammenschluss, like any other MdB's (ADR 0001). What they say while exercising the Sitzungsleitung is never a Rede and is excluded. That includes the turns that WP 18/19 XML prints inside another speaker's `<rede>`. Official addresses given on behalf of the Bundestag, for example a Gedenkstunde opened by the Präsident, are told apart from ordinary debate contributions and are not counted as Reden of a Fraktion automatically.

Decided 2026-10-20 by the project owner. The option, cross-checked with Codex, and its wording are the owner's. It was found in the A3 pilot ([a3-pilot.md](../plans/a3-pilot.md)). WP 20/21 XML marks the chair with a `<name>` element, which the parser already treats as Sitzungsleitung. WP 18/19 XML instead marks the chair inside a member's `<rede>` with a `<p klasse="redner">` marker whose `<rolle_lang>` is Präsident, Vizepräsident(in) or Alterspräsident. That covers, for example, Lammert's floor-giving in 18/100 and Solms and Schäuble at the constituent sitting 19/1.

## Considered Options

- **For the Fraktion (chosen).** CONTEXT.md already says a Präsident at the lectern "gives a Rede like any other Redner"; the office is held by an MdB of a Zusammenschluss.
- **A weitere Sprechrolle (rejected).** It would have treated the presidium as an organ of the Bundestag, like the Wehrbeauftragte, and taken their debate Reden out of their Zusammenschluss.

## Consequences

- `SPRECHROLLE_RULES` gets no presidium rule. A presidium role on a marker is evidence of Sitzungsleitung: it is never mapped to a side.
- The WP 18/19 extension of speech rule 5 classifies those markers as Sitzungsleitung (excluded). An official address on behalf of the Bundestag needs its own detection and treatment. Until that exists, such a turn is refused rather than counted as a Fraktionsrede.

## Amendment 2026-11-13: three kinds, a reviewed list, and the procedural turns

Decided by the owner as Q13 A (2026-11-06, [v1-scope-review.md](../plans/v1-scope-review.md)) and built from [adr-0004-presidium-kinds.md](../plans/adr-0004-presidium-kinds.md), whose census read every candidate in WP 18-21. A `<rede>` whose owner carries a presidium role is one of three kinds:

- **Fraktionsrede**: a Vizepräsident(in) printed with a Fraktion speaks for it (unchanged, `mark_presidium_fraktionsreden`).
- **Ansprache**: an address given for the Bundestag, or by a guest or another constitutional organ at a ceremonial or joint sitting. A Beitrag of Kind `ansprache`, shown with the printed role, counted in no Reden figure and for no side. One kind for presidium and guest addresses: their treatment is the same, and the role on the card says in what capacity the speaker spoke.
- **Sitzungsleitung**: chairing printed as a `<rede>` of its own. Not stored; recorded as a procedural turn.

The code cannot tell an address from a debate Rede or from chairing, so the kind of each case is reviewed data: `scripts/reviewed_units.json`, keyed by sitting and source `<rede>` id, with the owner's Redner-ID as the proof of identity. A third list kind, `procedural`, covers the acceptance of an election and the answer to an oath where the protocol prints them as the elected or sworn person's own `<rede>`, and (amendment 2026-11-21) a Fraktion's nomination in an election printed as the nominating MdB's own `<rede>`: they are no contribution to a debate (ADR 0002), and 82 of them were counted as Reden in the 718 kept sittings.

Nothing is counted unread. A presidium `<rede>` the list does not name (a Präsident(in) or Alterspräsident(in) printed with a Fraktion included, since only the Vizepräsident role is stripped), and a short `<rede>` that reads like an oath answer or an acceptance but is not listed, are withheld with the Rede named (reason `unreviewed_presidium`, ADR 0005); the sitting stays. The list is the one file ADR 0005 introduced for withheld units: a stale entry stops the run, as a malformed list does, because the list and not the source needs correcting, and an edit re-reads exactly the sittings it touches. A guest role no Sprechrolle rule maps still fails the Sprechrolle check unless the guest's `<rede>` is listed as an Ansprache.

## Amendment 2026-11-20: a Vizepräsident(in) at a Fragestunde marker

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-20, without a reviewed list entry. Built on branch `claude/rule-18-62-18-90-e926a3` for decision 4 of [t4-acquire.md](../plans/t4-acquire.md), which lets 18/62 and 18/90 leave the exclusion record. A Fragestunde prints no `<rede>`: each turn is a marker outside any `<rede>`, an MdB's a Frage and an official's an Antwort. A Vizepräsident(in) printed there with a Fraktion carried the presidium role, was read as an official, and failed the Sprechrolle check, because no rule maps the role.

- **Not chairing: the MdB's Frage.** A marker outside any `<rede>` whose `<rolle_lang>` is Vizepräsident(in) and which prints a Fraktion is that MdB's turn for the Fraktion. The role is removed, as `mark_presidium_fraktionsreden` removes it from a Fraktionsrede. This is Petra Pau's four Nachfragen (three in 18/62, one in 18/90).
- **Chairing: Sitzungsleitung.** When the last chair `<name>` before the marker ends with the marker's first and last name, that person presides, and a presiding member cannot ask from the chair. The marker opens no turn and its words are read as a `<name>` element's are. This is one marker: in 18/62 "Johannes Singhammer (CDU/CSU): Sie, Frau Kollegin, haben mit Sicherheit eine Nachfrage." stands between two of his `<name>` turns ([source-errors.md](../source-errors.md), E11).
- **What tells the two apart is who chairs, not the text.** Round 3 (2026-10-22) kept both sittings out because only the content seemed to separate Singhammer's turn from Pau's. The last chair `<name>` separates all five.
- **No reviewed list entry.** Decision 4 planned one entry per marker. `scripts/reviewed_units.json` names a unit by its `<rede>` id, and these markers have none; the chair check needs no list. The five markers are all there are: a scan of the 803 files held (the 801 of the reference store and the two excluded files) finds no other presidium marker outside a `<rede>`, with or without a Fraktion.
- **Unchanged:** a Präsident(in), an Alterspräsident(in) and a Vizepräsident(in) without a Fraktion keep their role at such a marker, so the Sprechrolle check still refuses them unread. A presidium marker inside a `<rede>` stays Sitzungsleitung (round 2); 18/113's Zwischenfrage by Pau is not covered (TODOS.md, "A presidium member's Zwischenfrage read as the chair").
- **Measured:** every unit of the 801 stored sittings parses to the same result with and without the rule. 18/62 goes from 62 Fragen and 69 Antworten of the Fragestunde to 65 and 65 (Pau's three move, Singhammer's turn goes), 18/90 from 39 and 39 to 40 and 38.
- **Limit:** the chair is taken from `<name>` elements only. A handover printed only as a presidium marker inside a `<rede>` would not be seen; none of the five markers follows one.

## Amendment 2026-11-20: a stage direction printed as `<name>`

Built on branch `claude/stage-direction-name-d93b24` from TODOS.md ("ADR 0004 follow-ups"). Approved by the owner on 2026-11-21. WP 20/21 XML marks a chair turn with a `<name>` element, and the parser reads everything after one as Sitzungsleitung until the next speaker marker. In 20/25 `ID202506700` the source prints a stage direction in that element, `<name>(Vizepräsident Wolfgang Kubicki räuspert sich)</name>`, and Lamya Kaddor's closing words after it were read as chair text ([source-errors.md](../source-errors.md), E12).

- **The rule:** a `<name>` with no child elements whose whole text, trimmed, is one pair of parentheses with no parenthesis inside is a stage direction. It is read as a `<kommentar>`, which no part of the parse reads as a turn, so the speaker before it goes on. `mark_stage_direction_names` applies it before anything else reads the tree.
- **Why parentheses alone:** the TODO entry also proposed "a `<name>` that names no speaker". The scan finds none: of the 110,346 `<name>` elements outside a `<redner>` in the 803 files held (the 801 of the reference store, 18/62 and 18/90), 110,166 are a role, a name and a colon; of the other 180, one is the 20/25 stage direction and 179 each name a person: chair labels with words after the colon or no colon (19/167, 19/180, 19/214, 21/58, 21/76, 18/230, 18/234), a split role ("Vizepräsiden t", 19/176), and the real speakers printed as `<name>` that TODOS.md "Speakers printed as `<name>` who are not the chair" tracks. A rule for a case no file holds would have no measured effect, so it is not built.
- **Untouched:** a `<name>` that starts with a label and holds a parenthesis (a constituency such as "Norbert Müller (Potsdam) (DIE LINKE) :", or an unbalanced opening parenthesis) keeps its reading.
- **Measured:** of the 806 files held (the 803 above and 21/107 to 21/109; replayed 2026-11-21), only 20/25 parses differently, and in it one unit: Kaddor's Rede goes from 16 to 18 paragraphs, 5,317 to 5,661 characters and 783 to 835 words (split on whitespace). Reden, Beiträge and withheld units are unchanged in every sitting (20/25: 115 Reden, 8 Beiträge). 20/25 is stored, so the speech rule goes from 9 to 10 and the store owes one `--offline --repersist`.

## Amendment 2026-11-21: a nomination printed as a `<rede>` is procedural

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-21 (decision O13 of [adr-0004-presidium-kinds.md](../plans/adr-0004-presidium-kinds.md), option B). A Fraktion's nomination for the Präsident's election, printed as the nominating MdB's own `<rede>` ("Die CDU/CSU-Bundestagsfraktion schlägt Herrn Dr. Wolfgang Schäuble vor."), is a motion in the election, not a contribution to a debate (ADR 0002), so it is listed as `procedural`, like the acceptance that follows it. The four in WP 18-21 are 18/1 `ID1800100100`, 19/1 `ID19100900`, 20/1 `ID20100700` and 21/1 `ID21101200`; built on 2026-11-22 as four `procedural` entries in `scripts/reviewed_units.json` ([o13-nominations.md](../plans/o13-nominations.md)). Measured by replaying the 806-sitting store: Reden 61,559 → 61,555, CDU/CSU −3, SPD −1. Volker Kauder's first stored Rede moves from the 18/1 nomination to his 18/2 Rede `ID1800200500`, and the Fakt `meistdiskutierter-vorgang` has no value for 2017-10 and 2025-03, whose most discussed Vorgang was the Präsident's election with the nomination as its one Rede; no published flag, rank or winner changes. Other corpora (Open Discourse, Plenarprotokolle.digital) count Kauder's 19/1 nomination as a contribution of its own; this dataset's Rede is the narrower debate contribution.

## Amendment 2026-11-22: speakers printed as `<name>` who are not the chair

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-21, all four questions of [name-tag-speakers.md](../plans/name-tag-speakers.md) section 4 as recommended (option S). WP 18 and 19 print 170 turns of 51 sittings under a `<name>` element, the chair's element, although the speaker is not the chair: whole Reden given the floor in Rednerliste form, Zwischenfragen, Befragung questions and answers, Fragestunde Zusatzfragen, oath answers, one guest's address and one remark. The parser read every `<name>` as chair text, so these 80,381 words were counted nowhere, and two Reden were withheld because a turn inside them was lost.

- **The rule:** a `<name>` is the chair's when its tag opens with a presidium role, ends with the name a presidium tag of the same sitting prints, or stands in parentheses (the stage-direction rule above runs first). Any other `<name>` prints a speaker. Each one has an entry in `scripts/reviewed_units.json`, kind `name_*`, keyed by the source `<rede>` that holds the tag (or `N<ordinal>`, the tag's position among the sitting's `<name>` elements, for a tag outside any `<rede>`) and the printed tag. A printed name is no proof of identity, so the entry carries the Redner-ID: the 145 entries were generated from the plan's TSV, which the owner took as the review.
- **What each kind becomes:** a Rede, Beitrag or own-words tag is rewritten into a speaker marker of the entry's Redner-ID, so the existing rules read it. A Rede splits from its host at that marker whatever the opening (`split_nested_reden`), or, outside any `<rede>`, becomes `<rede id="N<ordinal>">`. A listed Beitrag must get the kind the rules give it, or the run stops. An oath answer stays procedural (a diagnostic, never a marker: inside a listed procedural `<rede>` another speaker's turn would withhold it). Wolf Biermann's address at the 18/64 Gedenkstunde is one Ansprache with no Redner-ID. Bartsch's remark in 18/181 is stored nowhere.
- **Identity:** Wanka is bound to 999990003, Schavan to her MdB-Stammdaten id 11003836, and Blankau-Rosenfeldt, whom the Bundestag gave no Redner-ID, to DIP person 1828 (`dip:1828`).
- **Nothing is counted unread:** an unlisted non-chair `<name>` inside a `<rede>` withholds that unit (reason `unreviewed_name_speaker`); one outside any `<rede>` refuses the sitting. An entry no tag matches stops the run.
- **Measured** (every file of the 806-sitting reference store parsed before and after, 2026-11-22): 50 sittings change. WP 18 Reden 12,949 → 13,029 (+80, +73,265 words), WP 19 19,981 → 19,983 (+2, +1,511 words). WP 18 Beiträge: Befragung answers +33, Befragung questions +3, Fragestunde questions +6, Zwischenfragen +20, Ansprachen +1 (8,707 words, plus Biermann's 383). 18/6 `ID1800602300` and 18/36 `ID1803601400` are no longer withheld (3,054 words). The marker ordinals after a converted tag move up by one, which renames 6 nested Beiträge and the withheld unit `ID1802902700:2`, now `:2:2:2`. Speech rule 10 → 11, export format 21 → 22.
