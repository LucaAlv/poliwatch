# Design: Fakt der Woche — a facts table that makes the site's numbers comparable, one card per sitting week

## Problem Statement

The user's words: "I discovered plenarwatch.de. There is significant overlap with my project.
I want to work out what the distinguishing features of my project are. Plenarwatch gets people
on their website by posting their feed on Instagram. One thing I had in mind is leveraging the
SQL database we build much more. There are cool examples on the database.html page, e.g. 'Wer
hielt die meisten Reden?'. That page feels like an endpoint for developers rather than actual
users, but a new page that displays interesting facts from the database could be a cool feature
that I could also make Instagram posts from."

Two things are being asked: what distinguishes Bundestag-Puls from plenarwatch (and the rest of
the field), and what a reader-facing "facts" page looks like that also yields a weekly post.

## What Makes This Cool

Every reader-facing Bundestag site found in the landscape check ranks cross-sectionally: who is
most absent, most aligned, best rhetoric, *right now*, against peers in the current
Wahlperiode. None of them compares a value against its own history. Bundestag-Puls has 290
sittings across WP20 and WP21 (2022-01-27 to 2026-09-11), 35,239 speeches, 217 roll-call votes
with 154,015 individual MP votes, all joined in one SQLite file. So it can say the sentence
nobody else says: **"this week's closest vote is closer than 96 % of all roll-call votes in this
Wahlperiode"**, with the SQL and the protocol page as the receipt.

That sentence is the product. The weekly card is its first output; badges on dossiers and MP
pages are its second; a downloadable `facts` table is what lets other people build on it.

Positioning that fell out of the session: the core is **the joined, current parliamentary
record** (speeches + votes + agenda items + Drucksachen + proceedings + MPs, rebuilt weekly).
The headline claim is *joined + current + measured against history + receipt attached +
downloadable*. Not "transcripts as data": Open Discourse and CPP-BT own breadth (1949 onward)
and neither has votes or DIP joins. Not editorial posts (plenarwatch) and not MP scorecards
(PlenaryData).

## Constraints

- Python stdlib only, no package manager, no framework, no build toolchain (README promise).
- Output is a static site; the SQLite store is rebuilt by `scripts/build_dip_pulse_site.py`.
- Sitting week (ISO week containing sittings) is already the site's time unit on `puls.html`
  (`select_pulse_week`, `week_header_facts`, `pulse_html.week_stats`).
- The Daten export contract (gz SQLite + CSVs + `datenstand.json`) is settled and not reopened;
  new tables are added to it, existing columns are not changed.
- No public data release exists yet; the Daten page's 8-week pilot gate has not started.
- `mp_canonical` exists only in the distribution copy (`build_dip_pulse_site.py:1932`, comment
  at line 130: "Nur in der Verteilkopie"); MP-level facts need it in the live store.
- `parties.name` carries Python-list-repr duplicates that 909 `mps.party_id` rows point at
  (TODOS.md P1); any Fraktion-level fact is wrong until that is fixed.
- The site avoids gotcha formats; the neutral primary-source posture is a feature.

## Premises (agreed, one revised after the cross-model read)

1. The core is the joined, current parliamentary record; the headline claim is joined +
   current + measured against history + receipt attached + downloadable.
2. A `facts` table in the store is the product; the card, the badges and any later feed are
   consumers of it, never separate computations. It is exported through the Daten CSV contract.
3. (revised) Each metric yields one observation per sitting week. A week's observation is
   ranked only against prior weeks' observations (history strictly before the week, so a
   published card never changes). Baseline is the same Wahlperiode; when the WP has fewer than
   N prior weeks for that metric, fall back to all coverage and say so. A metric with too little
   history yields "noch nicht vergleichbar" and is ineligible. Highest percentile wins; ties are
   broken by a fixed metric order (`knappste-abstimmung` before `laengste-rede`; the
   registry's `tie_rank`). The Wahlperiode of a sitting week is derived from the protocols'
   `document_number` prefix ("21/94" → 21; `xml_header_json.wahlperiode` as the cross-check;
   verified 2026-09-19: WP20 = 201 protocols to 2025-03-18, WP21 = 89 from 2025-03-25) and
   stored on the fact row. The comparison population (count, date range, baseline kind)
   is stored on the fact row and printed on the card. The rule is published on a Methodik page.
4. Facts are longitudinal ("seltenste seit Beginn der Wahlperiode"), never cross-sectional
   gotchas; MP-level facts stay factual (first speech, longest speech, lone dissent), never
   attendance rankings.
5. The card is an SVG build artifact; rasterising for Instagram is a local macOS `qlmanage`
   one-liner outside the repo; no new dependency.
6. feed.json / RSS are deferred behind the Daten pilot gate. The facts tables ride the export
   mechanically, but no public data release exists yet, so until the Daten page's public-release PR (`scripts/publish_dip_pulse_data.sh`, TODOS.md
   "Scheduled data-release workflow") ships the first release
   the Fakt pages are the only surface anyone sees; once a release exists the facts CSVs are in
   it without further work and without a separate announcement.
7. Doing nothing is a real cost: no weekly reason to return, nothing post-shaped. Three of five
   comparable data projects found are dormant or degraded; the weekly card is what forces the
   next build.
8. `parties.name` is fixed first; MP-level facts join via `mp_canonical` as recipes R1/R3 do.
9. Open Discourse is a compatibility target, not a dependency: the export gains a view/CSV
   variant with their column names (speeches, contributions, politicians, factions,
   electoral_terms) so WP20/21 slots into existing notebooks. Their code (Python 3.7 +
   PostgreSQL + Docker, not under active development) and their pre-2017 corpus stay out of the
   repo; a history backfill is a later, separate decision.

Ruled out during the session (one line each):
- Leaderboard wall: evergreen rankings never change week to week; nothing to post.
- Parameterised query explorer: not post-shaped; explorers get built and not used.
- MP-vs-MP duel cards: editorial pair-picking, gotcha risk.
- Human shortlist pick / top-3 card / category rotation: weekly upkeep, or dodges the choice.
- Pillow or headless browser in the repo: breaks the dependency-free promise for one image.
- Datasette Lite as a reader (Codex suggestion): downloads a Python runtime; same size and
  dependency objections as sql.js.
- "Transcripts as data" as the headline: loses on breadth in the first sentence.

## Landscape (2026-09-19)

- Official open data: XML/JSON protocols since WP1, XLSX roll-call lists
  (bundestag.de/services/opendata).
- Research corpora: Open Discourse (900k speeches 1949–WP19, MIT, stalled), CPP-BT on Zenodo
  (1949–2025, Parquet/CSV), OffenesParlament (WP18, dormant), Bundestags-Mine (NLP, WP19–20,
  some analyses offline).
- Reader-facing WP21: plenarwatch.de (one LLM-drafted post per vote with six blocking checks;
  Muster page is cross-sectional for the current WP, no history; no downloads or API; Instagram
  distribution); PlenaryData / deepdataanalytic.de (per-MP scorecards: attendance, votes,
  Zwischenrufe, "AI rhetoric scores"); abgeordnetenwatch (votes per MP).
- Eureka logged: the longitudinal question ("is this week unusual?") is unoccupied, less
  gotcha-prone than scorecards, and needs the multi-WP per-sitting store only this project has.

## Cross-Model Perspective (Codex, cold read)

- Coolest version: a card that follows a proceeding through the plenary ("sixth return to the
  plenary; only X % of proceedings returned as often") opening a timeline of debates, speakers,

- Challenged premise 3 as underspecified: weekly values cannot be ranked against per-sitting

  change under a different baseline, the headline depends on the baseline. Accepted; premise 3
  revised as above.
- Existing tool: Datasette / Datasette Lite for the exploration half (declined, see above);
  the domain half (comparable windows, completeness checks, evidence, stable versions,
  readable cards) is the part to build.
- Weekend prototype: fix party normalisation; `scripts/facts.py` with `fact_metrics`, `facts`,
  `fact_sources`; two metrics (longest speech by `char_count`, closest roll-call vote by
  `ABS(yes-no)/(yes+no)`); emit `fakt/index.html`, `fakt/<week>.html`, `fakt/<week>.svg`,
  `fakt/methodik.html`; export through the Daten contract; move `mp_canonical` into a shared
  step before MP facts; `tests/test_facts.py` for ties, incomplete weeks, missing votes,
  future-history exclusion, reproducible selection.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Replay-first — engine, two metrics, one card, Methodik (chosen)

- Summary: the facts tables and the surprise rule, two sitting-week metrics, a replay flag that
  prints the last N weeks' winners, the Fakt page family and the SVG card, tables added to the
  export. Registry-shaped from day one so B is additive.
- Effort: M. Risk: Low.
- Pros: a post after one weekend; the replay proves or disproves the rule on real data before
  any page is designed; prerequisites are two known items.
- Cons: two metrics means many weeks look alike; no badges, so the distinguishing feature is a
  side page until the next PR; no MP-level facts yet.
- Reuses: `RECIPES` execution pattern (`build_dip_pulse_site.py` ~1408), `select_pulse_week` /
  `week_header_facts` (~3410–3543), `export_distribution_data` (~1776),
  `ComponentDefinition` / `NAV_ITEMS` (`scripts/features/__init__.py`), test fixtures in
  `tests/_daten_fixture.py`.

### Approach B: Site-wide layer — three metric families, badges, Open Discourse export view

- Summary: A's engine plus MP metrics (via `mp_canonical` materialised in the live store),
  proceeding metrics, badge hooks in the dossier and MP renderers ("in dieser Woche: knappste
  Abstimmung der Wahlperiode"), the Open Discourse-compatible export view, the Methodik page.
- Effort: L. Risk: Med.
- Pros: the full claim on every page; the export becomes the WP20/21 continuation of a dataset
  people already use; adding a metric becomes a small function plus a test.
- Cons: touches the dossier and MP renderers and needs a schema step before the first post;
  more live numbers before the rule is proven.
- Status: **not discarded; the full implementation A is the test run for.** Recorded in
  TODOS.md as the follow-up.

### Approach C: Proceeding-first cards with a timeline page (Codex's lateral)

- Summary: the weekly card is about the proceeding with the most unusual trajectory this week
  (returns to the plenary, speech volume across debates, final margin) and opens a timeline
  page joining debates, speakers, documents and votes.
- Effort: L. Risk: Med–High.
- Pros: uses the joins nobody else has; each card opens a story; the timeline page is the bill
  page TODOS keeps deferring.
- Cons: trajectories are censored at 2022 ("sixth return we observed"); proceeding titles are
  boilerplate (existing TODO); it is a bill page project first.
- Status: **not discarded; becomes the third metric family inside B once the engine is proven
  and a timeline/bill page exists.** Recorded in TODOS.md.

## Recommended Approach

**A now, shaped so B is the next PR and C is a metric family inside B.**

Design of A:

**Tables** (in the live store, written by the build, exported as CSVs):

- `fact_metrics(id, version, title, unit, direction, sql, min_history_weeks, tie_rank)`: the
  registry row per metric; `sql` is the per-week observation query, `direction` is `max` or
  `min`, `min_history_weeks` the minimum number of prior comparable weeks (N below).
- `facts(id, metric_id, metric_version, iso_year, iso_week, wahlperiode, value, denominator,
  baseline_kind, baseline_count, baseline_from, baseline_to, percentile, eligible, selected,
  source_snapshot)`: one row per metric per sitting week; `denominator` is `yes_count + no_count`
  for `knappste-abstimmung` and NULL for metrics without one (`laengste-rede`); `baseline_kind` is `wp` or `all`; `selected` marks the
  week's winner (unique per week, may be absent); `source_snapshot` is the datenstand hash the
  row was computed from.
- `fact_sources(fact_id, entity_kind, entity_id, official_url, page_anchor)`: the receipt rows
  (speech + protocol page, vote + bundestag.de detail page, Drucksache).

**Metrics in A** (one observation per sitting week each):

- `laengste-rede`: `MAX(speeches.char_count)` over the week's sittings with `mp_id IS NOT NULL`
  (an unattributed speech cannot be named on the card; none exists in the store today, the
  fixture in `tests/_daten_fixture.py` builds one); unit characters, never inferred speaking
  time. Card sentence (templated): "Die längste Rede der Woche: 14.812
  Zeichen von {display_name} ({Fraktion}), länger als 93 % aller Wochen-Spitzenreden seit
  Beginn der 21. Wahlperiode." It names the MP via `mp_canonical` and the Fraktion via
  `parties.name`, which is why both prerequisites below are blockers for A, not nice-to-haves.
- `knappste-abstimmung`: `MIN(ABS(yes_count - no_count) * 1.0 / (yes_count + no_count))` over
  the week's roll-call votes; raw counts kept; zero denominators excluded; a week with no
  roll-call vote yields no observation (missing, not zero). Card sentence: "Die knappste
  Abstimmung der Woche: 312 zu 309 zu {title}, knapper als 96 % aller namentlichen
  Abstimmungen seit Beginn der 21. Wahlperiode."

**Row-level receipt tie-break**: when two rows share the week's max/min, the row with the
lowest `speeches.id` / `votes.id` is cited in `fact_sources`; a vote linked to several
`vote_documents` cites the lowest `document_id` as its Drucksache and lists the others as
further `fact_sources` rows (no such tie exists in the store
today; the rule is for reproducibility).

**Rule** (premise 3), implemented once in `scripts/facts.py` and documented verbatim on
`fakt/methodik.html`.

**Pages**: `fakt/index.html` (archive, newest first, each card as `<img>` of its SVG plus the
sentence, the comparison population and the receipt links), `fakt/<iso-year>-W<ww>.html`
(one fact, its SQL, its sources, the comparison series as a plain list or a small inline SVG),
`fakt/<iso-year>-W<ww>.svg` (1080×1080 card: headline number, one sentence, comparison
population line, site name), `fakt/methodik.html`. Nav item "Fakten" gated by a new
`facts` component that requires `store`. A sitting week with no eligible metric (no roll-call
vote and too little speech history, expected only in the first weeks of WP20) still gets its
`.html` with the observations and the line "Kein Fakt der Woche: noch nicht vergleichbar",
no `.svg`, and a greyed row in the archive; a week is never skipped silently.

**Replay**: `--facts-replay N` prints a table of the last N sitting weeks with each metric's
value, percentile, baseline kind and the selected winner, using history-before-week only.
This is the first thing to run and the assignment below.

**Local raster script** (outside the repo, in the design doc only):
`qlmanage -t -s 1080 -o ~/Desktop .context/dip-pulse-site/fakt/2026-W37.svg`.

**Prerequisites** (both existing TODOS items): the `parties.name` fix and migration; moving
`mp_canonical` materialisation from the distribution-copy step into a shared step so the live
store carries it (needed for R1/R3 parity and for B's MP metrics).

## Open Questions

- Value of N (minimum prior comparable weeks before a metric is eligible). Proposal: 8.
  The replay will show how many WP21 weeks say "noch nicht vergleichbar" under 8 vs 12.
- Percentile definition when the week's value ties a prior value: rank strictly-better or
  better-or-equal. Proposal: strictly-better, so a tie with the record is not "100 %".
- Whether the card sentence is templated per metric in Python (deterministic) or drafted by the
  LLM step with the validators the summaries use. Proposal for A: templated; every card is
  reproducible byte-for-byte.
- German copy for the baseline line ("verglichen mit 41 Sitzungswochen seit Beginn der 21.
  Wahlperiode" vs "… seit Januar 2022").
- Whether `facts` rows for weeks before the engine existed are computed on first build (they
  are, under history-before-week they are reproducible) and whether the archive shows them.
  Proposal: compute all, show all; the archive is instantly 97 weeks deep.

## Success Criteria

- The 30-week replay selects a fact in at least 20 of the 30 weeks whose cited row (speech or
  vote) differs from the previous week's, and at most a third of the selected winners change
  when the baseline is switched from `wp` to `all` (Codex's test). If
  it fails, the rule is revised before any page is built.
- An offline rebuild writes `fakt/index.html`, one `.html` and one `.svg` per sitting week in
  the store, and `fakt/methodik.html`; `facts.csv.gz`, `fact_metrics.csv.gz`,
  `fact_sources.csv.gz` appear in the export with sha256 lines in the manifest.
- Rebuilding with the same store and `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` yields byte-identical cards.
- A test pins: history-before-week (a later week never changes an earlier row), missing-vote
  weeks, ties, ineligible metrics, the fixed tie-break, and the selected flag being unique per
  week.
- One card rasterised with `qlmanage` and posted.

Reviewed by /plan-eng-review the same day; the execution plan with all decisions is
`~/.gstack/projects/LucaAlv-poliwatch/fakt-der-woche-plan.md`. Where this doc and the plan
differ, the plan wins. What changed, in one line each:

- A is split: **A0** = read-only replay + sample cards, no writes (the test run); **A1** = engine
  in the build, pages, Methodik, export. A1 starts only if A0 passes.
- `mp_canonical` is not a prerequisite; receipts cite `(document_number, rede_id)` or the page
  anchor; MP links resolve via `mp_lookup` like recipes.
- New prerequisite instead: `speeches.fraktion` (affiliation on the day of the speech, from the

  (`sql_sha256` in `fact_metrics`), prints a changed-winners report; `source_snapshot` dropped;
  metric SQL errors abort the build; `--no-persist` never writes.
- Week completeness comes from the build's `entries` (vote acquisition state, XML parsed);
  incomplete weeks are excluded from facts and history and shown as "unvollständig erfasst".
- Percentile = strictly-beaten prior weekly observations, direction-aware; N = 8; WP baseline

- Vote week = protocol week via the R4 join.
- Card copy names its population ("… der wöchentlich knappsten Abstimmungen seit …") and says
  "seit Januar 2022" for WP20 (coverage starts at protocol 20/14); `facts.week_n` stored.
- The guarantee is "spätere Wochen ändern frühere Karten nicht; Datenkorrekturen können es";

- SVG: textwrap into tspans, ≤3 lines, system font stack, no filters.
- A0 pass criteria replace the Success Criteria above: no speaker on > 5 of 30 cards; each
  eligible metric wins ≥ 6 of 30; ≤ ⅓ of winners change between `wp` and `all`; winners vs
